   <h2 class="sec">What you get</h2>
   <p class="sub">The outputs of one simulation, and what it can model.</p>
   <div class="feat">
     <div><b>Phase PSD</b>In rad²·m², for each AO error term.</div>
     <div><b>Error budget</b>The integral of each PSD, in nm RMS.</div>
     <div><b>PSF</b>Long and short exposure, at any wavelength.</div>
     <div><b>Fiber coupling</b>Coupling efficiency.</div>
     <div><b>Fourier filtering WFS</b>Pyramid sensitivity and non-linearities with the convolutive formalism.</div>
     <div><b>Cascaded AO</b>The result of one stage feeds the next one.</div>
     <div><b>Light</b>Only needs numpy, scipy and astropy.</div>
     <div><b>Compatible</b>TIPTOP ini files in SCAO mode, and the OOPAO end-to-end tool.</div>
   </div>
