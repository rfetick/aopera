Temporal binning at one pixel

Pixel f = 5 λ/D along x (fx = 5/D = 0.625 m⁻¹), example 35-layer profile and loop (fs = 1380 Hz, ki = 0.15, delay 0.84 ms, D = 8 m). Each layer gives one entry at ft = fx·v·cos θ with power ∝ Cn²·|1/(1+OL)|². TemporalBins(nbin, fmax): bin 0 = [0, fmin[, then nbin−1 log-spaced bins from fmin = fmax/10⁶ to fmax. Bin power = exact sum, bin frequency = |PSD|-weighted mean.

cube entries (one per layer and per copy)bins (sum, weighted mean frequency)

The pixel variance is conserved exactly (it is a sum), whatever cube_bins. What changes is the frequency carried by each bin: when the cube feeds a next stage, its rejection is evaluated at this mean frequency instead of the individual ones. This is a small approximation on the next stage, and it has no effect on the PSD of the stage itself.
